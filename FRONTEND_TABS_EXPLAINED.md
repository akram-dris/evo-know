# 🖥️ EvoKnow Frontend Navigation Guide

This document provides an exhaustive, detailed breakdown of every navigation tab (view) available in the EvoKnow Cloud-Native Knowledge Management (KM) System's Vue.js frontend application. It explains the core purpose, the underlying AI microservice it interacts with, and the exact type of data and interactions expected within each view.

---

## 📊 1. Dashboard (Tableau de bord)
**Endpoint:** `/`
**Primary Role:** The central command center and high-level system overview.

*   **Description:** The Dashboard acts as the landing page for administrators and users. It is designed to provide a rapid, at-a-glance summary of the entire knowledge management ecosystem's health and activity.
*   **What it contains (Expected Data):**
    *   **High-Level Metrics:** Total active documents, total archived documents, number of pending conflicts, and system uptime status.
    *   **Activity Feed:** A brief timeline of the most recent system activities (e.g., "Document X ingested," "Report Y generated").
    *   **Quick Actions:** Shortcuts to upload new documents or trigger immediate system scans.
*   **Target User:** System Administrators, Knowledge Managers.

---

## 📈 2. T1: Prediction (T1 : Prédiction)
**Endpoint:** `/prediction`
**Associated Microservice:** `t1-prediction`
**Primary Role:** Proactive monitoring of knowledge decay and obsolescence.

*   **Description:** This view visualizes the output of the T1 Prediction Service. It identifies documents that are losing relevance over time or are actively becoming outdated based on access patterns and temporal decay models (like Prophet).
*   **What it contains (Expected Data):**
    *   **Obsolescence Risk List:** A prioritized list of documents flagged with high decay scores.
    *   **Score Breakdown:** Detailed metrics showing *why* a document was flagged (e.g., "Not accessed in 90 days," "Content similarity to newer documents is low").
    *   **Trend Graphs:** Visual charts mapping the historical access frequency of specific knowledge artifacts.
    *   **Actions:** Buttons for administrators to "Review," "Archive," or "Manually Update" the flagged documents.

---

## 📄 3. T2: Reports (T2 : Rapports)
**Endpoint:** `/reports`
**Associated Microservice:** `t2-report-generation` & Local LLM (`Ollama`)
**Primary Role:** Automated, AI-synthesized managerial summaries.

*   **Description:** This tab acts as a library for all automated, human-readable reports generated by the local AI model. Instead of parsing raw data, managers come here to read coherent summaries of system activities.
*   **What it contains (Expected Data):**
    *   **Report Archive (Left Column):** A chronological list of generated reports, categorized by type (e.g., `weekly_summary`, `consistency_report`, `fusion_report`, `discovery_report`).
    *   **Markdown Viewer (Right Column):** A rich-text reading pane that converts the AI-generated Markdown content into formatted HTML.
    *   **Content:** Reports detailing how many redundancies were merged, what new concepts were discovered, or general system health metrics over a specific period.
    *   **Actions:** Options to download reports as PDF or compare different versions over time.

---

## 🔀 4. T3: Fusion (T3 : Fusion)
**Endpoint:** `/fusion`
**Associated Microservice:** `t3-knowledge-fusion`
**Primary Role:** Managing semantic deduplication and content consolidation.

*   **Description:** This view provides transparency into the system's automated deduplication efforts. It shows where the AI has identified overlapping, redundant, or highly similar knowledge chunks across different documents and merged them.
*   **What it contains (Expected Data):**
    *   **Merge Events Log:** A list of recently completed fusion operations.
    *   **Before/After Comparison:** A visual diff tool showing the original disjointed chunks (Source A, Source B) and the newly synthesized, consolidated chunk.
    *   **Clustering Visuals (Optional):** Insights into how DBSCAN clustered similar documents based on cosine similarity before merging them.
    *   **Actions:** The ability for an administrator to review a fusion, approve it, or rollback/undo an automated merge if the AI consolidated incorrectly.

---

## 🛡️ 5. T4: Consistency (T4 : Cohérence)
**Endpoint:** `/consistency`
**Associated Microservice:** `t4-consistency-check`
**Primary Role:** The logical "Traffic Cop" ensuring factual integrity.

*   **Description:** This is a critical administrative view that highlights logical contradictions or structural anomalies within the knowledge base. It acts as an inbox for conflicting information that the AI cannot resolve autonomously.
*   **What it contains (Expected Data):**
    *   **Contradiction Inbox:** A queue of flagged issues (e.g., Document A states "Budget is $500", Document B states "Budget is $1000").
    *   **NLI (Natural Language Inference) Results:** Details showing the AI's confidence level that two statements contradict each other.
    *   **Graph Anomalies:** Alerts regarding structural issues in the Neo4j database, such as orphaned nodes or circular dependencies.
    *   **Actions:** Interface for human operators to manually intervene, override, supersede older data, or correct the identified discrepancies.

---

## 🧠 6. T5: Discovery (T5 : Découverte)
**Endpoint:** `/discovery`
**Associated Microservice:** `t5-knowledge-discovery`
**Primary Role:** Unearthing hidden relationships and extracting new ontological concepts.

*   **Description:** This view showcases the system's ability to learn and adapt. It displays new entities (NER) and hidden correlations (Association Rules) that the AI has mined from the ingested text, independent of human tagging.
*   **What it contains (Expected Data):**
    *   **New Entities List:** Newly discovered terms, departments, tools, or concepts extracted from raw text.
    *   **Association Rules:** Insights generated by algorithms like Apriori (e.g., "Documents mentioning 'Kubernetes' also frequently mention 'Helm' with 85% confidence").
    *   **Knowledge Graph Preview:** A visual representation of newly formed nodes and edges in the Neo4j database.
    *   **Actions:** Options to formally accept a newly discovered relationship into the core organizational taxonomy or reject false positives.

---

## 📚 7. Knowledge Base (Base de connaissances)
**Endpoint:** `/knowledge-base`
**Associated Microservice:** `api-gateway` & Local LLM (`Ollama`)
**Primary Role:** The primary end-user search and interaction interface.

*   **Description:** This is the core utility for standard employees. It combines traditional search with advanced AI capabilities to find and synthesize answers from corporate data.
*   **What it contains (Expected Data):**
    *   **Document Catalog:** A searchable, filterable list of all active knowledge artifacts in the system.
    *   **RAG Chatbot (AI Knowledge Assistant):** An interactive chat interface powered by the local `llama3` model. Users can ask natural language questions (e.g., "What is our policy on remote work?").
    *   **Citations & Confidence:** Responses from the chatbot explicitly cite the source documents (chunks) used to generate the answer, alongside a calculated confidence score to ensure trustworthiness.

---

## ⏱️ 8. Audit Log (Registre d'audit XAI)
**Endpoint:** `/audit`
**Associated Microservice:** `orchestrator`
**Primary Role:** Ensuring transparency, accountability, and Explainable AI (XAI).

*   **Description:** This view acts as the "black box recorder" for the entire system. It is crucial for compliance and understanding *why* the AI made specific autonomous decisions.
*   **What it contains (Expected Data):**
    *   **Chronological Event Table:** A strict ledger of every significant action taken by the orchestrator or microservices.
    *   **XAI Explanations:** For every automated action (e.g., auto-archiving a document, generating a report), a human-readable explanation is provided detailing the reasoning behind the decision.
    *   **Data Fields:** Timestamp, Action Type (e.g., `GENERATE_REPORT`, `DETECT_STRUCTURAL_ISSUE`), System Component, Detailed Explanation, and Verification Status.

---

## ⚙️ 9. Settings (Configuration)
**Endpoint:** `/settings`
**Primary Role:** Application and system preference management.

*   **Description:** An administrative panel to tweak the behavior of the frontend and potentially interface with backend configurations.
*   **What it contains (Expected Data):**
    *   **User Preferences:** Theme toggles (though currently standardized to a premium Light theme), notification preferences.
    *   **System Parameters (Future Scope):** Interfaces to adjust LLM model selection (e.g., switching from `llama3` to `mistral`), tuning confidence thresholds for automated actions, or managing user access roles.
